What this means: Shows if your sales are going up, down, or staying flat over time. Growth = getting better, Falling = needs attention, Stagnant = not moving much.

What this means: Your most recent quarterly sales and how much they changed from the previous quarter. Positive % = improving, negative % = declined.

What this means: Your compound annual growth rate. Shows your average year-over-year growth trend.

What this means: How unpredictable your sales are. Low = stable, High = jumpy. High volatility means lower confidence (but same target).
